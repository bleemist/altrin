Community Cohesion & Voice

Building Community Cohesion in Buliisa District

Community transformation cannot occur through top-down mandates alone; it requires deep-rooted local trust, open dialogue, and shared ownership. ALTRIN’s community outreach framework is designed to bridge the gap between educational institutions, civic leadership, and rural households across Buliisa District and the broader Albertine region.

Through our bi-weekly Saturday radio broadcasts on **Biiso FM Radio (2:00 PM – 3:00 PM)**, ALTRIN reaches thousands of listeners across Western Uganda. Hosted by founding members like **Mr. David Bingi**, these broadcasts serve as an open town hall where parents, teachers, and local leaders discuss critical education challenges, seasonal farming adjustments, and student retention strategies.

Simultaneously, our **ALTRIN Online Community Networks** connect local youth, school coordinators, and alumni into active digital support hubs, facilitating real-time resource sharing, mentorship advice, and event announcements.

In addition to intellectual engagement, ALTRIN mobilizes community members for practical action through organized **Community Cleanup Initiatives** in Biiso Town and local sub-counties. By working side-by-side to clear public spaces and plant trees, students and residents cultivate civic pride and environmental responsibility.
