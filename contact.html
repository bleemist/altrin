Stakeholder Engagement

Partnering for Lasting Community Impact

ALTRIN actively collaborates with school administrations, non-governmental organizations, rotary clubs, development partners, and community leaders across Western Uganda. Whether you represent an educational institution seeking learner tracking implementation, a corporate sponsor interested in funding student debate galas, or a volunteer educator, our team is eager to engage.

Our regional coordination team in Biiso Town processes school enrollment requests, mentorship registrations, and community cleanup sponsorship inquiries. You can reach out directly via our official phone lines, email, or by filling out the JotForm application form.
