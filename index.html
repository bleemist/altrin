Our Core Purpose

Pioneering Holistic Transformation Across the Albertine Region

The Albertine Transformation Initiative (ALTRIN) is a community-anchored organization operating in Buliisa District and across the Albertine region of Uganda. We bridge critical gaps in primary, secondary, and tertiary education by tackling the root causes of school dropouts, economic fragility, and environmental degradation.

Academic Excellence & Retention

Our customized Learner Tracking System (LTS) follows children from Primary 1 to University graduation, combining TaRL foundational literacy, career guidance, and academic counseling to prevent dropouts.

Household Economic Stability

We empower parents with micro-business incubation, Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLA), and sustainable agriculture training so families can reliably support their children's education.

Environmental Stewardship

We foster environmental conservation by establishing green clubs in schools, conducting tree planting campaigns, and organizing community cleanup drives to ensure climate resilience.

Regional Context & Insight

Understanding the Educational Ecosystem in Buliisa & Albertine Region

The Albertine region of Western Uganda is undergoing rapid socio-economic transition driven by infrastructural expansion, agricultural growth, and energy developments. However, educational institutions across rural sub-counties like Biiso face severe structural challenges, including high primary-to-secondary transition dropouts, acute learning poverty in foundational literacy, and socio-economic vulnerabilities among farming and fishing households.

Standard educational interventions often focus exclusively on classroom infrastructure or teacher ratios, overlooking the complex socio-economic realities of rural families. When parents experience income shocks or seasonal agricultural deficits, children are frequently withdrawn from school to support domestic labor or early employment.

ALTRIN was established to address these interconnected systemic barriers through an integrated, community-centric model. By combining continuous **Learner Tracking (LTS)** with targeted **Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL)**, we ensure that academic progress is actively guarded against learning loss and dropouts.

Simultaneously, by building parental economic self-reliance through Village Savings & Loan Associations (VSLA) and embedding environmental stewardship into local school clubs, ALTRIN creates a supportive ecosystem where educational achievement and community resilience reinforce one another.

Our Integrated Theory of Change

When learners are systematically tracked and supported academically, while parents are empowered economically and schools lead environmental conservation, rural communities break the cycle of poverty and achieve sustainable, generational transformation.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ALTRIN's primary focus in Uganda?
ALTRIN (Albertine Transformation Initiative) focuses on comprehensive educational tracking, foundational literacy/numeracy (TaRL), career guidance, parent economic empowerment, and climate action across the Albertine region.
How can schools or students join ALTRIN activities?
Schools and students can register through our online JotForm or contact our team directly via email or phone. We work with both prospective partner schools and community groups across Buliisa District.
What is ALTRIN Week?
ALTRIN Week is an annual flagship event bringing together secondary schools for inter-school debates, poetry recitations, essay writing, football tournaments, and professional career guidance sessions.
