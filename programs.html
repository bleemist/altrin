Our Flagship Initiatives

Empowering Minds, Transforming Futures

Explore our six interconnected programs driving academic retention, skills development, parental support, and environmental conservation.

Methodological Framework

How Our Interventions Connect

At ALTRIN, programs are not siloed activities; they function as a unified continuum. A child enrolled in our **Learner Tracking System** benefits from **TaRL foundational learning** in primary school, participates in **ALTRIN Week debate galas** in secondary school, receives **professional career mentorship**, while their household is reinforced by **parent economic empowerment** and **environmental stewardship**.

Program 01

Learner Tracking Systems (LTS)

The Learner Tracking System (LTS) is ALTRIN’s central data-driven framework designed to eliminate school dropouts across Buliisa District and the Albertine region. By establishing a longitudinal monitoring record for every child from Primary 1 through secondary education and university graduation, LTS identifies early warning signs of academic distress.

Our field teams work directly with school headteachers and class coordinators to track attendance patterns, termly academic performance, and socio-economic risk factors. When a learner demonstrates chronic absenteeism or falling grades, ALTRIN dispatches MEL field officers to conduct home visits, counsel parents, and arrange targeted academic or financial interventions before the child drops out permanently.

  • Early warning indicators for absenteeism, fee distress, and relocation.
  • Longitudinal academic trajectory mapping across P.1 to University.
  • Home visitation and school-parent reconciliation counseling.

P.1 to University Continuum

Educational transitions in rural Uganda—specifically the move from P.7 to S.1 and S.4 to S.5—represent the highest dropout periods. LTS provides dedicated transition counseling and academic bridging during these critical milestones.

Result: 95%+ retention rates among tracked learners across partner schools.

Remedial Foundational Mastery

Many primary school children reach P.4 or P.5 without mastering basic paragraph reading or simple subtraction. TaRL breaks this barrier by grouping children according to actual learning level rather than age or grade.

Rapid progression: Learners master foundational reading within 30-50 intensive session hours.
Program 02

Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL)

Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL) is a proven pedagogical methodology pioneered to tackle learning poverty in primary schools. Instead of following rigid curriculum paces that leave struggling pupils behind, TaRL evaluates basic reading and arithmetic skills using simple diagnostic tools.

ALTRIN trains primary school teachers and community instructors to conduct fun, interactive 1-hour daily sessions focusing purely on phonics, word building, number recognition, and basic operations. Through hands-on learning games and storybooks, pupils rapidly gain the foundational confidence required for upper primary subjects.

  • Simple diagnostic assessment tool for reading & math levels.
  • Level-based student grouping for accelerated learning.
  • Teacher capacity building and classroom mentoring support.
Program 03

Career Guidance & Mentorship

Students in rural secondary schools often lack exposure to diverse professional career paths and higher education requirements. ALTRIN’s Career Guidance & Mentorship program connects secondary learners and university undergraduates with established professionals across medicine, engineering, law, business, education, and public administration.

During school visits and ALTRIN Week galas, professional mentors—such as medical experts like **Mr. Ellison Tibaingana**—conduct interactive keynote sessions explaining subject combination choices, university admission procedures, job market trends, and professional ethics.

  • Secondary school career discovery workshops & subject mapping.
  • One-on-one mentorship matching for university undergraduates.
  • Resume building, interview prep, and leadership coaching.

Bridging Education to Industry

Mentorship instills self-belief and vision. By interacting directly with successful career role models from their own region, students gain the confidence to pursue ambitious academic goals and professional excellence.

Mentorship Assistant: Ms. Abisobora Liz coordinates mentor matching across schools.

Financial Security for Education

When parents achieve financial stability, school fees are paid on time, children receive proper nutrition and learning materials, and domestic stress is significantly reduced.

VSLA Impact: Hundreds of household education funds established across Buliisa sub-counties.
Program 04

Parent Economic Empowerment

Educational retention cannot succeed in a vacuum of household poverty. The Parent Economic Empowerment initiative strengthens family livelihoods by training parents in micro-enterprise development, financial literacy, and sustainable agricultural practices.

ALTRIN facilitates the creation of Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLA) where parents pool weekly savings, access low-interest micro-loans for small business ventures, and establish dedicated education emergency funds to protect their children's schooling during agricultural downturns.

  • Micro-business skills incubation & financial management training.
  • Village Savings & Loan Association (VSLA) group setup.
  • Climate-smart agriculture & income diversification support.
Program 05

Environmental Stewardship & Climate Action

Environmental degradation and climate variability directly impact rural livelihoods and school stability in the Albertine region. ALTRIN’s Environmental Stewardship initiative mobilizes schools, youth, and local communities to champion environmental conservation and climate resilience.

We establish Environmental Clubs in partner primary and secondary schools where students manage tree nurseries, plant indigenous shade and fruit trees across school grounds, and learn practical environmental conservation. We also organize town cleanup initiatives in Biiso to promote waste management and sanitation.

  • School tree nursery establishment & compound re-afforestation.
  • Community cleanup campaigns & waste reduction drives.
  • Climate literacy curriculum integration & youth eco-clubs.

Nurturing Green Schools

By involving pupils directly in tree planting, children develop lifelong environmental responsibility while transforming bare school compounds into shaded, eco-friendly learning environments.

Try our interactive "Plant a Tree" game on the Home page!

Inter-School Academic Gala

Launched in October 2023 at Biiso Sub-County Hall, ALTRIN Week brings together secondary schools like Biiso War Memorial S.S, St. Francis S.S, Mukitale Development Foundation S.S, and Kihungya Seed S.S for public speaking, debates, and athletic galas.

Sports Officer: Mr. Businge Gerald coordinates football tournaments & athletic galas.
Program 06

ALTRIN Week & Inter-School Competitions

ALTRIN Week is our flagship annual event that celebrates youth talent, formal argument skills, creative writing, and athletic teamwork. The week-long sessions feature intense inter-school debates, poem recitations, essay competitions, brain-teasing academic games, and a friendly football tournament.

Winning students and participating schools receive certificates of excellence, trophies, and academic materials during a grand awards ceremony attended by district education officials, teachers, and community members.

  • Formal argument & public speaking debate competitions.
  • Poetry launch, essay writing & creative arts showcase.
  • Inter-school football gala & team-building sports tournament.
